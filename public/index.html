    Guest data

    Removes everything NexOS saved in this browser for guest mode: tasks, habits, notes and focus history. Download your data first if you want to keep it.

    Delete account data

    Permanently deletes your tasks, habits, notes, focus history, NexAI chats and reminder subscriptions. This cannot be undone. Your Google name, email and photo record stays on the server so you can sign in again; ask the site owner if you want it removed too. Type DELETE to confirm.
