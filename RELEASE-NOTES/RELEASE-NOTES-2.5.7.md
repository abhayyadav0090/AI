# NexOS 2.5.7: Better first day

## Added
- **Guest insight:** "Get insight" on Classic Home now works in guest mode. It gives a short suggestion worked out on this device (overdue task, top-priority task, open habits) instead of asking you to sign in.
- **First-day steps:** The Productivity score card (Classic and Enterprise) shows "0 of 3 first-day steps done" until you check off a habit, finish a task and complete a focus session.

## Changed
- **Plan my day (Classic):** Task blocks use your focus timer length (for example 50 minutes for Study) and breaks use your short-break length. Tasks with their own estimate keep it. Guests see a note that NexAI scheduling needs a Google sign-in.

## Fixed
- **Enterprise Insights showed invented numbers.** The Productivity score was a fixed 88, the focus chart showed made-up minutes for Monday to Saturday, the task chart invented a segment for each empty column, and the earlier weeks of the completion chart were made up. All now show your real data. The score uses the same formula as Classic, and the focus chart shows the last 7 days from your focus log.

## Notes
- No stored data changed, so there is nothing new to sync between tabs.
- Enterprise has no completion history stored yet, so the earlier weeks on its completion chart are left empty.
