# NexOS 2.5.9: A shorter Settings page

## Changed
- **Settings now opens as a short list.** Look, Experience, Reminders, Your data and Delete are folded rows. Each row shows its current value (for example "Apple · Dark" or "On, daily at 21:00"). Tap a row to open it.
- **Your setup moved to the top,** right under your name, with the Change... button.
- **Look is smaller.** The two big design preview cards are now two buttons (Google, Apple) next to Light, Dark and Match device.
- **One data row.** Download my data, Restore and Recently deleted are together. The separate Export button is gone, because Download my data already includes everything Export had, plus your NexAI chats. Restore still reads these files.
- **Reminders:** the time and Save button only show while reminders are on. Switching reminders on or off now saves straight away.
- **Delete account data** is a folded row that always opens closed.
- **Save name** appears only after you change the name.

## Added
- **Home setup card for existing accounts.** If you never answered the quick-start question, Home shows a one-time card with the four choices and Not now. It changes only the focus timer and Home order, never your tasks, habits or notes. Not now is remembered, and you can still use Your setup in Settings.

## Notes
- No server change and no change to stored data.
- Guest mode keeps its own Guest data row. Name, Reminders and Delete account data stay hidden for guests, as before.
